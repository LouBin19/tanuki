Calling all Raccoons!

Well, maybe we shouldn't invite the Raccoons. Did you know a Tanuki is not a Raccoon? Even though they are called a Raccoon dogs? A Tanuki is actually in the Canidae family. Yeah, they are related to Wolves and Foxes.

They also hibernate, and are the only ones in the Canidae family to do so. Which is kind of neat, if you like to take the term Lazy Dog seruously.

Their species is also old AF. They are considered a basal species. Basically something most similar to it's ancestors. This means dogs probably looked more like Tanukis than woleves... Don't tell the Huskies.
